## iOS Storefront Freeze

Before changing any iPhone/iOS storefront UI, read:
`IOS_STOREFRONT.md`

Canonical iPhone styles:
`src/styles/platform/ios-storefront.css`

Visual contract:
`src/styles/platform/ios-storefront.contract.ts`

Do not modify the iOS storefront contract unless the task explicitly requests an iPhone/iOS visual change.
Android/Desktop changes must not modify the iOS contract.
